Kata: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Kata translates text on your own device. This policy explains what that means for your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Kata has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Your text

Text you translate (typed into the popup, selected on a page, or typed into a text box) is processed by a translation model running inside your browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The extension only reads a web page when you ask it to, by using the right-click menu or a keyboard shortcut, and then only the selected text or the text box you are typing in.

What is stored on your device

  • Translation models you download, stored in the browser's cache for this extension. You can delete them at any time from the extension's settings page, or by removing the extension.
  • Your settings (chosen languages), stored with chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome may sync across your own signed-in browsers.
  • Your last popup text, stored locally so it isn't lost when the popup closes.

Network requests

The only network requests are downloads of model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), made when you download a model or first translate a language pair. These requests contain the model's name and no text you translate. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to those downloads.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
